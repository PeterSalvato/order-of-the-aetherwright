The sigil is the whole system drawn as one mark. It is a seal: it marks a document, a tool or a work as belonging to the Order. It records nothing. Recording is the work of the Codex.

## Construction

The eight domain glyphs stand in a fixed wheel. `●` stands at the centre. Chevrons may be set around the wheel to mark the phase a work is in. The sigil as drawn on the cover of this tract is the canonical form.

## Positions

| Position | Glyph | Domain |
|----------|-------|--------|
| Top | `▲` | Systems |
| Top right | `■` | Design |
| Right | `▶` | Illustration |
| Bottom right | `⬢` | Code |
| Bottom | `▼` | Narrative |
| Bottom left | `⬟` | Photography |
| Left | `◀` | Intuition |
| Top left | `◆` | Craft |
| Centre | `●` | Study |

## Use

The sigil is set on covers, seals and stamps, and at the head of a document. It is never required.
